Why we started

Getting an official photo still means finding a photo booth or a studio, paying for prints and hoping they are accepted. We think that should take a few minutes, at home.

Every country has its own rules for size, background, head position and expression. SelfIDeed knows those rules and checks your photo against them, so you know it is right before you send it.

What we care about

  • Precision

    Exact sizes and rules for every document, shown plainly. Precision is what gets photos accepted.

  • Privacy

    Your photo is used only to make your document photo. No account, and we never sell your data.

  • For everyone, everywhere

    One simple service for people in 64 countries, whatever document they need.
