Questions and answers

Will my photo be accepted?

We check every photo against the official rules for your document: size, background, head position, lighting and expression. If something is wrong, we tell you what to change. The final decision is always made by the authority that issues your document.

Which documents and countries do you support?

Passport, ID card and visa photos for 64 countries. See all countries.

Do I need to install an app?

No. SelfIDeed works in the browser on your phone.

How much does it cost?

You can try it for free. The price for your document is shown in the app before you pay, in your own currency.

How do I take a good photo?

Stand about an arm's length from a plain, light wall, facing a window. Hold the phone at eye level, look straight into the camera and keep a neutral expression. Take off your glasses and keep your hair away from your face.

Can I print my photo?

Yes. You get a digital file for online applications, which you can also print at any photo shop.

What happens to my photo?

We use your photo only to make your document photo. Our privacy policy explains how long we keep it and how to have it deleted.
